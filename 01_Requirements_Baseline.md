# Requirements Baseline

## 1. Project Overview

**Project:** AI-Assisted Employee Leave Management — Requirements Analysis & Process Transformation

**Project Type:** Hypothetical Business Analyst case study

This case study explores how a standardized employee leave-management workflow could improve process consistency, request-status visibility, and centralized record maintenance. AI is used to support the initial drafting of user stories and acceptance criteria, followed by human review, requirements validation, gap identification, and acceptance criteria refinement.

## 2. Problem Statement

The hypothetical company's leave-management process relies on multiple communication channels for leave requests and manual maintenance of leave records. This fragmented process may create challenges in request tracking, status visibility and record consistency. The case study will explore how a standardized, centralized workflow could address these potential issues and improve leave-management efficiency.

## 3. Business Objective

To standardize the employee leave submission and approval process through a consistent workflow, centralized leave records, and clear request-status tracking to improve process consistency and visibility while reducing avoidable manual effort.

## 4. Requirements Baseline

### 4.1 Business Requirement

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BR-01 | Improve leave-processing efficiency and consistency by reducing avoidable manual effort and improving request-status visibility. |

### 4.2 Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | The employee shall be able to submit a leave request through the standardized workflow. |
| FR-02 | The manager shall be able to review a submitted leave request and approve or reject it. |
| FR-03 | The employee shall be able to view the current status of their leave request. |
| FR-04 | HR shall be able to maintain centralized leave records. |

### 4.3 Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Access to employee leave information shall be appropriately secured. |
| NFR-02 | The proposed system shall provide reliable availability for intended users. |
| NFR-03 | Leave-request status changes shall be reflected and communicated promptly. |

## 5. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Employee leave-request submission through a standardized workflow.
- Manager review and approval or rejection of submitted requests.
- Employee visibility into leave-request status.
- Centralized leave-record maintenance by HR.
- Drafting and reviewing user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Identifying assumptions, gaps, clarification questions and traceability between requirements and acceptance criteria.

### Out of Scope

- Leave balances, entitlements and eligibility rules.
- Development of a chatbot or AI application.
- Database design and implementation.
- Request cancellation, editing or resubmission.
- Escalation, delegation or multilevel approval workflows.
- Mandatory rejection reasons, audit-history features or specific notification channels unless subsequently agreed as requirements.

## 6. Key Stakeholders and Their Roles

| Stakeholder | Expected Role |
|---|---|
| Employee | Submits leave requests and views request status. |
| Manager | Reviews submitted requests and approves or rejects them. |
| HR | Maintains centralized leave records. |

These roles are defined for the hypothetical case study and have not been validated through actual stakeholder interviews.

## 7. Assumptions and Clarification Needs

The following details require clarification before the proposed requirements can be considered fully testable:

- Mandatory information required when submitting a leave request.
- Valid request-status values and status-transition rules.
- Expected timing for status updates and communication.
- The communication method and intended recipients for status changes.
- User roles, permissions and authorization rules.
- Permitted HR record-maintenance actions.
- Availability targets, service hours and measurement methods.
- Measurable indicators for evaluating leave-processing efficiency and consistency.

These are open questions, not confirmed business decisions.

## 9. Limitations

This is a hypothetical documentation-focused case study. No actual company stakeholders were interviewed, and no system was implemented or tested. Business benefits have not been measured. Some requirements remain subject to clarification, particularly measurable availability and response-time expectations.

The purpose is to demonstrate a structured Business Analyst approach to requirements documentation, traceability, AI-assisted drafting and human validation—not to claim a deployed solution or verified business results.
